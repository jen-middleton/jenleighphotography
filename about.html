About

Hi, I'm Jen.

I'm a Colorado photographer who lives for the hour before sunset. I'm based in the foothills near Denver, and I photograph couples, engagements, seniors, and weddings across the Front Range and up into the mountains, on both film and digital. My favorite moments are the in-between ones, the real laugh, the quiet look, the way two people settle into each other when they forget the camera is there.

My style is warm and relaxed. We pick a location that feels like you, chase the good light, and I'll guide you when you need it, so mostly you just get to relax and enjoy the evening.

When I'm not behind the camera, I'm usually planning my next scuba trip. I'm a landlocked Coloradan who is a little obsessed with the ocean, and that love of light and color finds its way into everything I shoot.

I believe life deserves to be captured beautifully, the big days and the ordinary golden evenings alike. If that sounds like you, I'd love to hear from you.
