Senior Photos in Conifer, Colorado

Aspens, meadows and pines close to home, a local guide to senior photos in Conifer and the 285 corridor.

I'm Jen, a senior photographer based right here in Conifer, Colorado. If you're a senior at Conifer High School or anywhere along the 285 corridor, you don't need to drive down to Denver for beautiful senior photos. Some of the best backdrops in Colorado are already in our backyard: aspen groves, open meadows, ponderosa pines, granite boulders and mountain views, all within a few minutes of home.

Favorite senior photo spots in and around Conifer

Meyer Ranch Park sits right off Highway 285 and is a local favorite for good reason. There are meadows near the trailhead, aspens and pines up the trail, and soft, even light under the trees.

Flying J Ranch Park is a quieter Conifer park off Highway 73, with open meadows ringed by ponderosa pines and aspens. It's a great choice if you want room to move without many people around.

Reynolds Park, just south of Conifer on Foxton Road, has a creek, rocky outcrops and old ranch history. Perfect if you want something a little more rugged.

Staunton State Park, about 15 minutes up 285, has huge granite cliffs and big mountain views. A Colorado state parks pass is required to enter.

Elk Meadow Park in nearby Evergreen has golden meadows and old wooden structures. Several of the photos on this page were taken there, and it's only about 20 minutes from Conifer.

The best time of year for senior photos in Conifer

Late summer and fall are my favorite. The meadows turn gold in August and September, and the aspens usually peak in late September to early October. Spring brings green grass and wildflowers by June. Up here at over 8,000 feet the seasons run a little behind Denver, and we can get snow from October through May, which makes for some really fun winter senior photos if you're up for it.

Golden hour in the mountains

I schedule most senior sessions in the last hour or two before sunset. In Conifer the sun drops behind the ridges a bit earlier than it does in Denver, so we usually start a little sooner. That warm, glowing light coming through the pines is the look you see in most of these photos. In the summer, afternoon storms roll through often, and we can always reschedule if the weather turns.

What to wear

Bring two or three outfits: something casual like jeans and a simple top, something a little dressier, and something that feels most like you. Earthy tones, creams, soft neutrals, rust and deep greens look beautiful against the pines and golden grass. Wear comfortable shoes for walking on trails, and bring your nicer shoes to change into. A cozy sweater or jacket is perfect for fall sessions, since it cools off fast up here once the sun goes down.

Why choose a local Conifer photographer

Because I live here, I know which trails are quiet in the evening, where the light hits best at each park, and how fast the weather can change. I can scout a location the week of your session and plan around the conditions. And you can stay close to home instead of driving to the city.

Frequently asked questions

Where are you located?
I'm based in Conifer, Colorado, and photograph seniors in Conifer, Evergreen, Morrison, Littleton, Denver and across the foothills.

When should I book my senior photos?
Most seniors book in the summer or fall before graduation. Fall dates go fast, so I recommend reaching out a month or two ahead.

Can I bring a friend, a pet or a sibling?
Yes. Friends, siblings and pets are always welcome. Just let me know ahead of time so we can plan for it.

Can we do my session somewhere special to me?
Absolutely. Your favorite trail, your family's land or a spot that means something to you all make great locations.
