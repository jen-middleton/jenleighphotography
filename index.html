About

I am Jen, a Colorado photographer who lives for the hour before sunset.

I photograph couples, engagements, and portraits across the Front Range and up into the mountains, on both film and digital. My favorite work happens when people relax into each other and the light does the rest, so if your kind of day is warm evening light, honest laughter, and somewhere beautiful, we will get along.

Based in the foothills near Denver, I shoot all across Colorado. Favorite spots include Washington Park in Denver, Evergreen Lake and Elk Meadow in Evergreen, Lookout Mountain in Golden, Red Rocks in Morrison, Guanella Pass, and the Flatirons in Boulder. I am always happy to travel for the right location.
