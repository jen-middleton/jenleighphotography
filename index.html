About

I am Jen, a Colorado photographer who lives for the hour before sunset.

I photograph couples, engagements, and portraits across the Front Range and up into the mountains, on both film and digital, and I am now booking weddings and elopements. My favorite work happens when people relax into each other and the light does the rest, so if your kind of day is warm evening light, honest laughter, and somewhere beautiful, we will get along.

Based in Colorado, and happy to travel for the right story.
